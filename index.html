Show Your Support for Meals on Wheels!

Add our ExtraGive frame to your Facebook profile picture. It's quick and easy—and you don't need a photo-editing app.

1. Choose Your Photo

Choose the photo you'd like to use for your Facebook profile picture.

2. See the Frame Added

Your photo will automatically appear underneath the ExtraGive frame.

3. Download Your Profile Picture

4. Update Your Facebook Profile Picture

  1. Go to your Facebook profile and tap or click your profile picture.
  2. Select Update profile picture.
  3. Select the new picture you just downloaded.
  4. Adjust the picture if needed.
  5. Tap or click Save.

You're all set! Thanks for helping spread the word about ExtraGive and Meals on Wheels of Lancaster.
